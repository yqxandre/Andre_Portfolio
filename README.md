# [Project 1: Modelling Student Productivity: A Statistical Analysis of Digital Habits and Academic Behavior](https://github.com/yqxandre/Statistical-Analysis-of-Digital-Habits-and-Academic-Behavior.git)

This is a project I did as part of my course, where we did a statistical analysis of a Digital Habits and Academic Behavior dataset
- Data was taken from a Digital Habits and Academic Behavior found on Kaggle
- **Models Used**: Linear Regression, Lasso
- **Report includes**: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Regression Modelling, Model Diagnostics, Regularization & Variable Selection
- **Key Findings**: Identified study hours, sleep duration, phone usage, stress levels, attendance, and focus as key predictors retained by LASSO. The analysis also highlighted the limitations of interpreting statistical relationships without considering the underlying data-generating process.


# [Project 2: Product & Sales Inventory Tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1K-aLP9Bv1lPVUMEAizF1JCNbUB08VfmQGapdy2KvTxY/edit?gid=321918386#gid=321918386)

This was part of a community service project that aided an upcycling business in Vietnam.
- **User-Centred Design**: Developed a product and sales inventory tracker in Google Sheets, prioritising ease of adoption and long-term maintainability for users transitioning to a new system.
- **Data Structure & Management**: Designed an intuitive data-entry workflow that balanced familiarity for users with the need for consistent, structured data suitable for downstream analysis.
- **Dashboard & Performance Metrics**: Enhanced the tracker with dashboards and performance metrics to provide clearer insights into inventory and sales performance.
- **Key Challenge**: Bridge the gap between a familiar spreadsheet interface and an analytics-ready data structure, ensuring that the system remained accessible to users without compromising its analytical potential.
